Compiler, HPC, and Systems Venues: A Cheat Sheet

A list of venues I keep an eye on for work on compilers, programming languages, software engineering, HPC, computer architecture, and systems for machine learning. I keep it here mostly for myself. If you also follow security and cryptography venues, MPC Deadlines Hub is a great tracker with exact deadlines and CORE ranks.

Ranks come from ICORE2026 (CORE2023 when ICORE2026 gives no letter rank), and each rank links to its CORE entry. Each table is sorted by rank. Deadlines and conference months are approximate and follow recent editions, so always check the official call for papers.
